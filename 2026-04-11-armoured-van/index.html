    Critical Market Insight¶

    There are effectively NO armoured CIT vans available for private purchase on the UK domestic market. This is not a search failure — it is a market reality:

    1. UK security companies (G4S/Allied Universal, Loomis, Brinks) dispose of decommissioned CIT vehicles through specialist dealers
    2. These specialist dealers (primarily Gericia International in London) are contractually or regulatorily restricted from selling CIT vehicles within the UK or Ireland
    3. All UK-based CIT vehicles are export-only
    4. The mainstream used vehicle platforms (AutoTrader, eBay, Gumtree) have zero armoured vehicle listings

    The realistic options are: - Buy from Gericia for export (their Sprinters are in London but marked "cannot be sold into UK/Ireland" — since the vehicle goes to Ukraine, export purchase may be possible) - Import from Europe (Germany has the most active market for used Geldtransporter/CIT vans)

    Armor Level Reference¶

    Level Protection Rounds Stopped Relevance to Ukraine
    B4 Handgun .44 Magnum, 7.62x25mm Tokarev Limited — shrapnel/pistol only
    B6 Assault rifle 7.62x39mm AK-47, 7.62x51mm NATO Highly relevant — standard threat in conflict zone
    B7 Armor-piercing rifle 7.62x51mm AP Maximum practical protection

    For Ukraine evacuation, B6 is the minimum useful level. B4 protects against handguns and some shrapnel but not rifle fire.

    Conversion Considerations¶

    Converting a CIT van to passenger use requires: 1. Remove safes and cash-handling equipment from rear compartment 2. Install seats and seatbelts — the rear compartment has no seating 3. Add ventilation/heating to rear — CIT vans have sealed rear compartments 4. Emergency exit — CIT vans often have only one entry/exit point 5. Windows are NOT feasible — cutting through armored panels is extremely difficult and compromises protection. Passengers would ride in an enclosed compartment (acceptable for short evacuation runs) 6. Weight consideration — armored vehicles are significantly heavier than standard vans. The Fiat Ducato B6 likely weighs 3.5-4 tonnes empty.
