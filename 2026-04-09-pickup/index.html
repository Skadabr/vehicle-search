    Scoring System (Revised 2026-04-09)¶

    Year and mileage are the most important factors — they are objective, verifiable, and directly predict remaining useful life. MOT data is treated asymmetrically: bad results (when found) are trustworthy and disqualify, but clean MOT history is NOT proof of condition — only a physical inspection can confirm that.

    Factor Weight Scoring
    Year (age) 25% Newer = better. Objective, can't be faked.
    Mileage (absolute) 25% Lower = better. Verifiable via MOT mileage progression.
    Price (value) 20% Lower = better. Budget operation.
    Engine reliability 15% Based on engineering data, not one tester's opinion.
    MOT red flags 10% Disqualifier when bad. Mild positive when clean. Not proof.
    Location 5% Closer to Brighton = easier to inspect/collect.

    Key Observations¶

    Pattern: UK Hiluxes are structurally rotten at this price point¶

    All three Toyota Hiluxes in this search that were MOT-checked had catastrophic structural corrosion: - GF11 THG (2011, 1 owner, full history): sills corroded through, seat belt anchor holed - SM61 OLO (2011, 10 service stamps): chassis in poor condition, sills holed, all body mountings corroded - WU07 TXL (2007): brake pipes corroded throughout, fuel tank strap rotted off

    Toyota engines are mechanically excellent (2KD-FTV is gold standard) but the bodies do not survive UK salt exposure. At the sub-£5,500 price point, any Hilux old enough to be affordable is old enough to be structurally compromised.

    L200s fare better structurally¶

    • DG56 ORJ (2006, 20 years old): NO structural corrosion in 26 MOT tests
    • KY62 VOH (2013): NO structural corrosion in 8 tests
    • The 4D56 engine is proven and reliable, though not quite as legendary as the 2KD-FTV

    Isuzu D-Max: no double cabs in budget¶

    One D-Max found under budget (2015, £4,850, Gumtree WSM) but it's extended cab, not double cab. The only double cab D-Max found was the Cat S write-off (£5,250, disqualified). All clean double cab D-Maxes remain over £6,000.
