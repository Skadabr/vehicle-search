    Ground Clearance Comparison¶

    Platform Vehicles CarsGuide (mm) Manufacturer (mm) Verdict
    Renault Trafic Gen 2 (X83) AE14SVG, SY12TXD — ~160 HIGHEST — best for rough roads
    Citroen Dispatch Gen 2 (Sevel Nord) GRZ8768 150 ~150 Good — higher than Vivaro/Transit
    Renault Trafic III / Vivaro (X82) DS68SYG, AF67LWH, DU65MKG 127 ~160 Mid-range — CarsGuide may measure differently
    Ford Transit Custom (V362) SV15HZM, LK14BGU 118 ~150 LOWEST — worst for rough roads

    Key finding: The Citroen Dispatch does NOT have the lowest ground clearance — it's actually 150mm, higher than the Transit Custom (118-150mm) and comparable to the Vivaro/Trafic (127-160mm). The old-shape Trafic Gen 2 (SY12TXD, AE14SVG) has the best clearance at ~160mm.

    Note on measurements: CarsGuide measures consistently across brands (good for relative comparison) but may measure at the lowest body point (bumper/aero). Manufacturer specs typically measure at the lowest mechanical point (axle/sump). Actual clearance under load with 9 passengers will be lower than unladen specs.

    Notes & Observations¶

    1. Market is tight for Trafic-size 9-seaters under £6k. Most are £7k+ with reasonable mileage. The best value is coming from eBay/Gumtree rather than AutoTrader dealers.

    2. The Citroen Dispatch is the standout find. At £3,750 with 92k miles and a perfect MOT, it's exceptional value. The PSA 2.0 HDi engine is proven and has massive remaining life. The timing belt is the main item to verify.

    3. The R9M 1.6 CDTi engine appears across all 3 Vivaros. This engine is acceptable but known for timing chain issues at high mileage. The Dispatch's 2.0 HDi is arguably more robust.

    4. Worcester dealer "lightwoodvans" has two Vivaros — the 2015 and 2017. Both are ex-commercial use. They may negotiate on price, especially if buying both or offering cash.

    5. Ford Transit Custom minibuses are all £9k+ in this size class. Completely out of budget.

    6. VW Transporter shuttles were not found under £6,600 in 9-seat configuration.

    7. Nissan NV300 (KS17OHD) added via user-submitted eBay listing. Same platform as Trafic/Vivaro (R9M engine). Attractive on paper (2017, under budget, clutch done) but 224k miles with a 50% MOT failure rate during 55k mi/yr commercial use. Dangerous defects (exposed tyre cords, brake pads run to zero) indicate neglectful maintenance. Ranks #9 at 53/100.
